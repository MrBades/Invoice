About Yeedem Books

Yeedem Books is a lightweight, mobile‑first invoicing platform built for MSMEs in Nigeria. It enables businesses to create, share, and manage invoices entirely offline or on slow 3G connections. All data is stored securely on the server and end‑to‑end encrypted for compliance with the 2026 FIRS data standards (TIN, 7.5% VAT, sequential invoice numbering, and NRS clearance status).

The platform offers:

  • AI‑guided onboarding that asks only the essential business details.
  • Instant PDF generation with optional watermarks for guest invoices.
  • Dynamic WhatsApp and email sharing, automatically prompting for missing contact details.
  • HTMX‑driven UI components for a fast, reload‑free experience.

Our mission is to eliminate the friction of financial paperwork for small businesses, allowing them to focus on growth while staying compliant with Nigerian regulations.
