About Yeedem Books

Empowering Nigerian MSMEs with AI-driven financial tools.

Our Mission

Yeedem Books is designed to bridge the digital gap for small and medium enterprises in Nigeria. Our goal is to make professional invoicing, debt tracking, and business insights accessible to every market trader and service provider, even in areas with fluctuating network coverage.

AI-First Approach

With YB AI, you can generate an invoice in under 30 seconds simply by typing what you sold. Our hybrid engine ensures that you can continue working even when offline, with smart local parsing and automatic syncing when you're back online.

FIRS 2026 Compliant

Yeedem Books is built from the ground up to comply with Nigerian financial standards, including automatic VAT calculation and sequential invoice numbering.
